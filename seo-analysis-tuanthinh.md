### 1.5 Root Cause Analysis

**Why tuanthinh.net is not ranking:**
1. **Domain authority is too low** - insufficient quality backlinks
2. **Possible indexing issues** - Google may not be fully crawling/indexing the site
3. **New or recently rebuilt website** - may still be in Google's sandbox period
4. **Weak backlink profile** - only found on 1 third-party directory
5. **Content may lack E-E-A-T signals** (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)
6. **Competitors have years of head start** in SEO and content marketing

1. **Google Search Console setup** (most urgent)
2. **Meta description and OG tag implementation**
3. **Google Business Profile optimization**
4. **Aggressive content marketing** (2+ articles/week)

With consistent execution of this strategy, tuanthinh.net can realistically achieve **top-10 rankings for 20+ target keywords within 6 months** and establish a strong competitive position in the Binh Duong sheet metal fabrication market.
